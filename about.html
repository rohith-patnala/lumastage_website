Our point of view

The room comes first. Always.

The best system is not the one with the longest spec sheet. It is the one that fits your room, your listening habits and your life.

We approach each project as a collaboration between interior, architecture and engineering. That means asking the right questions early, being clear about trade-offs and keeping the details coordinated.
