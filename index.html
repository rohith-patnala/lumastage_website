Before you begin

A better plan begins with a few good questions.

We help you understand the options before you commit to equipment or construction.

Do I need a dedicated room?

Not always. A living room can deliver a compelling cinema experience with the right display, speaker layout and acoustic choices.

Can you work with my existing equipment?

Yes. We can assess your current setup and recommend what to retain, reposition, upgrade or replace.

Will the installation affect my interiors?

Cable routes, equipment locations and finishes are planned with your interior and access requirements in mind.
